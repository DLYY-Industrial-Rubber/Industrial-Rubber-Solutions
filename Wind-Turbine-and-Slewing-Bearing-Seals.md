# Engineering Guide: Wind Turbine Slewing Bearing Seals & Main Shaft Sealing Systems

Wind turbines operate under extreme environmental conditions, exposed to dynamic mechanical loads, severe temperature fluctuations, ultraviolet radiation, and continuous ozone attack. Maintaining effective sealing on the **yaw bearing**, **pitch bearing**, and **main shaft bearing** is essential to prevent grease leakage and protect internal roller components from ingress of water, dust, and coastal salt spray.

**Dalian Yingyu Co., Ltd.** (DLYY) manufactures high-performance elastomeric profiles and reinforced rotary seals specifically engineered for wind energy applications and heavy-duty industrial slewing bearings.

---

## 1. Primary Wind Energy Sealing Profiles

Dalian Yingyu provides custom-molded and continuous extruded elastomeric profiles tailored to specific wind turbine platform designs:

1. **Yaw Bearing Seals**: Installed along the tower-nacelle slewing ring to maintain internal grease lubrication during nacelle rotation while repelling external atmospheric contaminants.
2. **Pitch Bearing Seals**: Flexible seals fitted between the rotor hub and blade root, engineered to accommodate high dynamic flexing and micro-vibrations during blade pitch adjustments.
3. **Main Shaft Seals**: Heavy-duty rotary shaft seals designed to protect the main driveline bearing against continuous high-velocity rotation and thermal expansion.

## 3. Structural Design & Installation Features

* **Fabric-Reinforced Heel**: Incorporates high-strength textile fabric reinforcement at the seal heel to prevent deformation under pressure and ensure secure seating in the bearing housing groove.
* **Integrated Spring Retainers**: Stainless steel garter springs can be integrated into rotary lip designs to ensure constant radial contact force against moving surfaces.
* **On-Site Joint Splicing**: Vulcanized joint kits and custom-molded corner connections permit seamless field replacement without requiring complete disassembly of the wind turbine rotor or nacelle.
