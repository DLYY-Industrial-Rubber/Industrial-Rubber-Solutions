# Engineering Specification: High-Performance Rubber Waterstops & Hydrophilic Sealing Systems

In major civil engineering projects—such as hydro-electric dams, subway tunnels, underground basements, water treatment plants, and culverts—concrete expansion and construction joints are susceptible to high water pressure seepage. 

**Rubber Waterstops** manufactured by **Dalian Yingyu Co., Ltd.** (DLYY) are engineered from premium vulcanized elastomers (EPDM, Natural Rubber, and Neoprene) to accommodate continuous joint movement, thermal contraction, and structural settling while maintaining a completely watertight barrier.

---

## 1. Key Types & Design Profiles

Depending on the joint movement and installation method, Dalian Yingyu provides several standardized and custom-engineered waterstop profiles:

1. **Centerbulb Waterstops (Internal / Dumbbell Type)**: Designed for expansion joints with multi-directional movement. The center bulb deforms under concrete shifting without tearing or stretching the elastomeric web.
2. **Flat Ribbed Waterstops**: Ideal for construction joints with minimal shear movement, offering high mechanical bond strength with poured concrete.
3. **External / Surface-Applied Waterstops**: Mounted along the outer face of concrete structures prior to backfilling, widely used in basement slabs and retaining walls.
4. **Bentonite & Hydrophilic Swelling Waterstops**: Swells upon contact with water (up to 300% volume expansion) to seal micro-cracks and construction cold joints.

## 3. On-Site Joining & Vulcanization Methods

To form a continuous water-tight loop across complex rebar structures, proper joining techniques are essential:

* **Hot Vulcanized Splicing**: Using portable heating irons and raw vulcanizing rubber strips to create a seamless bond equal to 90%+ of original tensile strength.
* **Cold Adhesive Bonding**: Used for non-critical temporary waterproofing joints.
* **Prefabricated Factory Joints**: Factory-molded T-joints, L-joints, and cross-intersections delivered directly to job sites for hassle-free installation.
