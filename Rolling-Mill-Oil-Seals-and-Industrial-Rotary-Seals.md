# Engineering Specification: Heavy-Duty Rolling Mill Oil Seals & Industrial Rotary Sealing Systems

In high-load metallurgical processing, hot/cold rolling steel mills, and heavy industrial gearboxes, rotary seals operate under extremely demanding environments. Dynamic shaft runout, extreme thermal fluctuations, aggressive cooling emulsion ingress, and continuous scale contamination severely test seal integrity.

**Dalian Yingyu Co., Ltd.** (DLYY) manufactures high-performance **Rolling Mill Oil Seals** (including DF/HD and ACS series) and specialized textile-reinforced rotary seals engineered for extreme heavy-duty industrial machinery.

---

## 1. Core Rolling Mill Seal Series & Structural Designs

Dalian Yingyu provides engineered rotary sealing configurations tailored to specific metallurgical mill stand roll necks and drive assemblies:

1. **DF (HD) Series Heavy-Duty Seals**: Engineered with textile-reinforced elastomeric heels and flexible sealing lips. Designed to maintain continuous contact pressure on high-speed roll necks while withstanding severe structural deflection.
2. **ACS Series Rolling Mill Seals**: Advanced composite elastomeric seals featuring integrated secondary dust/emulsion exclusion lips. Prevents steel scale and cooling water from penetrating into the primary lubrication chamber.
3. **Textile-Reinforced Rotary Seals**: Fabric-backed elastomer constructions designed for press-fit installation in split bearing housings, offering high dimensional stability without peripheral leakage.

## 3. Structural Design Features & Installation Advantages

* **Split Type Design Available**: Select profiles are available in split-ring configurations for on-site maintenance, enabling quick seal replacement without dismounting heavy roll bearings or mill shafts.
* **Integrated Garter Springs**: Corrosion-resistant stainless steel garter springs maintain uniform radial load across shaft surfaces during dynamic thermal expansion.
* **Emulsion Exclusion Lips**: Dual-lip and triple-lip geometries effectively block high-pressure cooling sprays and abrasive mill scale.

---

## 4. Key Application Fields

* **Metallurgical Industry**: Hot strip mills, cold rolling mills, section mills, and continuous casting roll necks.
* **Heavy Engineering**: Industrial gearboxes, heavy mining machinery, and cement kiln drives.
* **Marine & Energy**: Large propulsion shafting and heavy rotary indexing equipment.
