## 1. Overview of Bridge Bearing Systems

Bridge bearings are critical structural components inserted between the bridge superstructure (girders) and substructure (piers/abutments). They accommodate structural movements—including thermal expansion, concrete shrinkage, creep, and traffic load deflections—while transmitting vertical and horizontal forces safely to the foundations.

### Key Types Manufactured by Dalian Yingyu Co., Ltd.:

1. **Laminated Elastomeric Bearings (Neoprene/NR)**: Constructed from alternating layers of vulcanized high-grade rubber and high-tensile internal steel reinforcement plates.
2. **PTFE Slide Elastomeric Bearings**: Integrated with a Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) sliding surface against a stainless steel plate to handle large horizontal displacements.
3. **Pot Bearings & Spherical Steel Bearings**: Designed for heavy-duty highway viaducts and railway bridges requiring multi-directional rotation and high load capacity (exceeding 10,000 kN).

## 3. Structural Expansion Joint Systems

Expansion joints protect bridge deck structures by allowing continuous traffic flow across gaps while accommodating thermal movement and sealing out water and de-icing chemicals.

### Product Range:
* **Single-Gap Strip Seal Expansion Joints**: Ideal for movements up to 80 mm. Features extruded steel edge profiles with a durable EPDM/CR rubber sealing element.
* **Modular Expansion Joints**: Engineered for multi-directional movements exceeding 80 mm to 800+ mm on large-span suspension and cable-stayed bridges.
* **Finger-Type & Steel Plate Expansion Joints**: Heavy-duty continuous steel surface joints designed for high-tonnage freight corridors.

## 4. Quality Assurance & Testing Standards

To ensure long-term structural safety (30+ years design life), every batch undergoes strict factory acceptance testing (FAT):

* **Compressive Proof Load Testing**: Verifies ultimate load-bearing capacity without bond failure or edge bulges.
* **Shear Modulus Verification**: Ensures correct stiffness response under lateral braking and seismic forces.
* **Ozone & Accelerated Aging Resistance**: Guarantees zero surface cracking under exposure to harsh environmental ozone and UV light.
